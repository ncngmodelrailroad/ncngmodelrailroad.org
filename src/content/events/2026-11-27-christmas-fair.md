## Visiting the Layout

The model railroad opens during the [Country Christmas Faire](https://www.nevadacountyfair.com/p/country-christmas-faire), November 27 to 29, 2026, at the Nevada County Fairgrounds.

**Layout hours: TBA.** Check this announcement for posted opening dates and hours before visiting. The host event's schedule is not the layout's schedule.

Bring the family to the railroad building to watch the On3 scale layout run in its winter setting.

## About the Faire

The Faire opens the holiday season with craft and gift vendors across the fairgrounds buildings.

The Nevada County Fairgrounds runs the Country Christmas Faire and posts vendor and admission details on its official site.

- **Faire info:** [nevadacountyfair.com/p/country-christmas-faire](https://www.nevadacountyfair.com/p/country-christmas-faire)

Check the Nevada County Fairgrounds for hours, admission, and parking.
