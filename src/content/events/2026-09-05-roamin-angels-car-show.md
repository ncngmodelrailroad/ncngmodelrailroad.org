The model railroad opens during [Cruisin' the Pines](https://roaminangels.com/about-the-show/), the Roamin' Angels Car Club show at the Nevada County Fairgrounds, on Saturday, September 5, 2026. The show fills the grounds with pre-1972 American classics, muscle cars and trucks, motorcycles, vintage trailers, and classic boats.

## Visiting the Layout

Find us in the railroad building inside the fairgrounds. Our open hours for the day are still being confirmed, so check back before you visit.

The car show is a quieter day than the Nevada County Fair or the Draft Horse Classic, so it is a good chance to talk with our volunteers about the layout, the history of the Nevada County Narrow Gauge Railroad, and how to get involved.

## About the Show

The Roamin' Angels Car Club runs Cruisin' the Pines at the fairgrounds. Admission, gate hours, and parking are set by the show, so check their site before you come.
