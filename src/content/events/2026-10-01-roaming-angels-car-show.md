The model railroad opens during the [Roamin' Angels Car Club](https://roaminangels.com/) car show at the Nevada County Fairgrounds. The Grass Valley club restores classic cars and runs car shows across the year, including its **[Cruisin' the Pines](https://roaminangels.com/cruisin-the-pines/)** show at the fairgrounds.

## Visiting the Layout

Stop by the railroad building to see the On3 scale layout run between the rows of classic cars. Our open hours can vary, so check back for confirmed times.

## About the Show

The Roamin' Angels Car Club sets the show date and entry details on its official site.

- **Club and show info:** [roaminangels.com](https://roaminangels.com/)

Check the Roamin' Angels Car Club for the current show date, registration, and schedule.
