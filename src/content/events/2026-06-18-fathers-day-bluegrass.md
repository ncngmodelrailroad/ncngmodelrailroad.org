The model railroad will be open during the **[Father's Day Bluegrass Festival](https://californiabluegrass.org/cbaevent/fathers-day-festival/)**, presented by the California Bluegrass Association at the Nevada County Fairgrounds, June 18–21, 2026. This is our next scheduled open house for 2026!

## Our Open House Hours

**Saturday, June 20:** 12:00 PM – 8:00 PM (confirmed)  
**Friday, June 19:** Hours to be announced

Stop by the railroad building between sets to see the On3 scale layout in action. Hours may shift with festival activity, so check back closer to the date.

## About the Festival

The Father's Day Bluegrass Festival is a four-day celebration featuring over 30 bands across multiple stages, plus camping, workshops, and youth programs. The festival is a separate, ticketed event run by the California Bluegrass Association — a festival ticket or fairgrounds admission may be required to reach our display.

- **Festival info:** [californiabluegrass.org/cbaevent/fathers-day-festival](https://californiabluegrass.org/cbaevent/fathers-day-festival/)
- **Tickets & passes:** [Buy festival tickets](https://californiabluegrass.org/cbaevent/fathers-day-festival/tickets/)

Please refer to the California Bluegrass Association for official festival schedules, lineup, ticketing, and camping details.
