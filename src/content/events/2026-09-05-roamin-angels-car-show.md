The model railroad opens during the [Roamin' Angels Car Club](https://roaminangels.com/) **[Cruisin' the Pines](https://roaminangels.com/cruisin-the-pines/)** car show at the Nevada County Fairgrounds on Saturday, September 5, 2026. The Grass Valley club moved the show to Labor Day weekend for 2026 because the fairgrounds booked out its usual September dates.

## Visiting the Layout

Stop by the railroad building to see the On3 scale layout run between the rows of classic cars. Our open hours can vary, so check back for confirmed times.

## About the Show

Cruisin' the Pines drew about 400 show entries and 2,000 visitors in 2025. The Roamin' Angels Car Club restores classic cars and posts registration and schedule details on its official site.

- **Club and show info:** [roaminangels.com](https://roaminangels.com/)
- **Show details:** [Cruisin' the Pines](https://roaminangels.com/cruisin-the-pines/)

Check the Roamin' Angels Car Club for entry registration, the day's schedule, and parking.
