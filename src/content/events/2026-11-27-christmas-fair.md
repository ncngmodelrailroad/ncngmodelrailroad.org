The model railroad opens during the [Country Christmas Faire](https://www.nevadacountyfair.com/p/country-christmas-faire), November 27 to 29, 2026, at the Nevada County Fairgrounds. The Faire opens the holiday season with craft and gift vendors across the fairgrounds buildings.

## Visiting the Layout

Bring the family to the railroad building to watch the On3 scale layout run in its winter setting. Our open hours can vary across the weekend, so check back for confirmed times.

## About the Faire

The Nevada County Fairgrounds runs the Country Christmas Faire and posts vendor and admission details on its official site.

- **Faire info:** [nevadacountyfair.com/p/country-christmas-faire](https://www.nevadacountyfair.com/p/country-christmas-faire)

Check the Nevada County Fairgrounds for hours, admission, and parking.
