The model railroad opens during [Cruisin' the Pines](https://roaminangels.com/about-the-show/), the Roamin' Angels Car Club show at the Nevada County Fairgrounds, on Saturday, September 5, 2026. The show fills the grounds with classic cars and trucks, motorcycles, vintage trailers, and classic boats.

## Visiting the Layout

Find us in the railroad building inside the fairgrounds. The car show runs 9 AM to 4 PM, and we plan to be open through show hours. Our exact hours depend on volunteer turnout, so check back before you visit.

Our volunteers run the trains and answer questions about the layout, the history of the Nevada County Narrow Gauge Railroad, and how to join the club.

## About the Show

The Roamin' Angels Car Club runs Cruisin' the Pines at the fairgrounds. The show and the fairgrounds set admission, gate hours, and parking, so check before you come.
