The model railroad opens during [Cruisin' the Pines](https://roaminangels.com/about-the-show/), the Roamin' Angels Car Club show at the Nevada County Fairgrounds, on Saturday, September 5, 2026. The show fills the grounds with classic cars and trucks, motorcycles, vintage trailers, and classic boats.

## Visiting the Layout

Find us in the railroad building inside the fairgrounds. We are open 9 AM to 4 PM, matching the car show hours.

Our volunteers run the trains and answer questions about the layout, the history of the Nevada County Narrow Gauge Railroad, and how to join the club.

## Admission and Parking

The car show and the fairgrounds handle admission and parking. Here is what they list for this year:

- General admission: $5.00 per person
- Kids under 12: free with a paying adult
- Active and reserve military with ID: free
- Parking: $10 per vehicle, paid at the fairgrounds gate and separate from admission

Gate 1 offers permit and disabled parking, and Gate 4 offers general and disabled parking. Both are $10 per vehicle without in-and-out privileges. Lots off fairgrounds property are privately owned and operated.

Dogs on a leash are welcome under fairgrounds rules. Please keep them clear of the cars and clean up after them.

## About the Show

The Roamin' Angels Car Club runs Cruisin' the Pines at the fairgrounds. The show and the fairgrounds set gate hours, so check before you come.
