# Open the Layout for Your Event

*A one-pager for event organizers at the Nevada County Fairgrounds.*

The Nevada County Narrow Gauge Historical Model Railroad is an all-volunteer
group founded in 1986 to preserve local rail history. Our layout is a permanent
display at the Nevada County Fairgrounds, and we are glad to open it to the
public during fairgrounds events. If you are organizing an event here and would
like the layout open, a few basic needs help make that possible.

## For our volunteers

- **Event admission for volunteers.** Complimentary event admission for the
  volunteers who staff the layout during your event.
- **Volunteer parking access.** Parking close enough to allow setup, shift
  changes, and a safe end-of-day departure.

## For our visitors

- **Inside the event footprint.** Our building kept within the active event
  footprint, so attendees can find and reach us rather than being cordoned off
  from the flow of the event.
- **A modest suggested donation.** Permission to accept a small suggested
  donation toward upkeep from visitors who wish to give.

## How we operate

- We staff the layout with trained volunteers during posted hours.
- We follow the applicable Fairgrounds and event safety, setup, and cleanup
  requirements.
- We keep the space family-friendly and welcoming to all ages.

## Mutual benefits

- A family-friendly attraction that adds value and dwell time to your event.
- A tie to local heritage that draws train enthusiasts, families, and history
  lovers.
- Community goodwill and cross-promotion between your event and our historical
  group.

## Get in touch
